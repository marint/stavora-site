Privacy

Updated 25 November 2026

Stavora does not collect, store or send personal data.

What the app handles

The app opens only the PDF files you choose or open in it. They are copied into the app's own storage on your device. Pen markings, crops, setlists and settings are stored in the same place.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking.
  • No network connections: your scores and markings never leave the device.

Sharing

When you share a setlist, the app makes a ZIP file and opens the device’s own share sheet. The file goes only where you send it, for example to Drive, email or WhatsApp. Stavora sends it nowhere.

Deleting data

Deleting a score in the app also deletes its markings. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. Files are chosen with the system's own file picker, which gives the app access only to the files you pick. The pitch pipe and metronome only play sounds: the microphone is not used.
