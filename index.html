For choir singers and musicians

More music. Less paper.

Stavora trims the empty margins from your PDF scores and straightens crooked scans so the music fills the screen. Mark it with a pen and turn pages with a tap or a pedal – in rehearsal, on stage or practising at home. Share a setlist with its markings with the whole choir in one tap.

See the features Coming to iPad and Android tablets, works on phones too

Made for rehearsals and concerts

In rehearsal the music has to be big, the markings have to stay, and the page has to turn without fumbling. Everything else comes second.

Automatic cropping

Empty margins are trimmed on import, scanned scores included. Notes written in the margins stay, hole punches and specks go, and grey scanned paper turns white.

Setlists

Put the concert programme together by dragging. After a piece's last page comes the next piece, and the corner shows what's next.

Share with the choir or band

The setlist, scores and markings in one package, and updates as markings only. Everyone’s own markings stay.

  • Passages to practise Mark with the flag what needs work. The library lists those scores with one tap and opens each at the right page.
  • Pen markings Write straight onto the score: four colours, a highlighter, text notes, an eraser and undo. With an active stylus you just start writing; your finger turns the page. The original PDF is never changed.
  • Pitch and metronome The starting note from a keyboard and a metronome with tap tempo, floating over the score, with the silent switch on too.
  • Page-turn pedals Bluetooth pedals and keyboards turn pages: arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Space and Enter.
  • From files and the cloud Pick several PDFs at once from the device or a cloud service such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox. You can also open a PDF from another app straight into Stavora, or bring in a whole folder as a ZIP.
  • Tablet and phone Designed for a tablet in portrait, and works on a phone too. In landscape a tablet shows two pages; a phone moves on one line of music at a time.
For choirs and bands

The whole choir on the same page

The conductor marks the passages to practise and shares the setlist with the markings. Everyone gets them in their own scores, and their own markings stay their own.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

While you read, only the music is on screen. Tap the middle of the page for the tools, or hold a finger still for a moment to start marking.

Two pages at a time

Turn the tablet to landscape to see two pages side by side and turn less often. Your markings come along.

A whole concert as one score

A setlist puts its pieces one after another like a single booklet, so the page turns the same way all evening.

  • Straight to the next piece. Forward from a piece's last page opens the next piece; back from its first page opens the end of the previous one.
  • Jump anywhere. The toolbar lists every piece and page.
  • Easy to build. Pick the pieces in the order you want and reorder them by dragging. A folder imported as a ZIP becomes a setlist right away.

Light or dark

Stavora follows your device, or you choose. Around the page stays as you set it – dark on stage, if you like.

Getting started

No accounts, no sign-up.

Import your scores

Pick PDFs from the device or a cloud service, or open them from another app straight into Stavora.

Read and mark

The score comes cropped. Mark what the conductor says.

Build a setlist

At the concert, turn from piece to piece with a tap or a pedal.

Your scores stay with you

Stavora runs entirely on your device.

  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • PDFs and markings are stored on the device only. They leave it only when you share them yourself.
  • PDFs are drawn by the operating system's own PDF engine.
  • A backup is one ZIP file that you save wherever you like.

Coming soon

Stavora is in testing on iPad and Android tablets. It works on a phone too, for the day the tablet stays at home. It will be in the stores soon.
